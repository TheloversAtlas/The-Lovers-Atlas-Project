CITIZEN REGISTRY

Privacy

This starter privacy notice is provided for the V2 prototype and should be reviewed before a public commercial launch.

Information collected

The Citizen Registry can collect your name, email address, registration date, entry destination, campaign/source information, Citizen number, passport status and whether you chose to receive email updates.

How it is used

Registry information is used to document Citizen identity, operate Atlas access and experiences, understand campaign performance, and—only where permission has been given—send Citizen Dispatches and project updates.

Email choice

Registering as a Citizen does not require opting into promotional email. Email updates can be enabled separately through the registration checkbox.

Contact and deletion

Before launch, replace this paragraph with the official privacy contact email and instructions for requesting access to or deletion of personal information.
